Corvane Biotherapeutics Reports Positive Topline Results from Phase II ORCA-2 Trial of CVB-114 in Refractory Atopic Dermatitis

Primary endpoint met with statistical significance; company to request End-of-Phase II meeting with FDA

Corvane Biotherapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: CRVB), a clinical-stage company developing oral immunology therapies, today announced topline results from ORCA-2, a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase II trial of CVB-114 in adults with moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis who had an inadequate response to prior systemic therapy.

At week 16, 58.4% of patients receiving CVB-114 200 mg once daily achieved EASI-75, compared with 21.7% of patients receiving placebo (p<0.001). The key secondary endpoint, vIGA-AD 0/1 at week 16, was also met.

CVB-114 was generally well tolerated. The most common adverse events were nasopharyngitis, headache and nausea, the majority of which were mild. Discontinuations due to adverse events were 3.8% in the CVB-114 arm and 3.2% in the placebo arm.

Corvane plans to present full results at an upcoming medical meeting and will request an End-of-Phase II meeting with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration in the first half of 2027.

REMEDIATION

How to prevent this

FIX 1
Accept comments and track changes before distribution
Word's "Accept All Changes and Stop Tracking" permanently removes w:del, w:ins, and w:rPrChange nodes from document.xml. Comments must be deleted separately — accepting changes does not remove them. Run both before any external send.
FIX 2
Convert to PDF via a headless pipeline, not by opening Word
A PDF rendered from the accepted-changes body contains no revision XML. It cannot carry w:del or comment nodes because those structures do not exist in the PDF format. Mandate PDF-only external distribution for embargoed documents.
FIX 3
Inspect the XML before sending any .docx
grep -r "w:del\|w:comment\|w:ins" word/ inside the unzipped .docx reveals every hidden revision node. A one-line CI check on any outbound .docx can block files that still carry tracked changes or comments.
